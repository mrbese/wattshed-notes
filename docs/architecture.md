# Architecture at a glance

WattShed is arranged around an evidence-to-decision path rather than a generic rebate form.

```text
Program terms and geography
            ↓
Public program design and homeowner intake
            ↓
Evidence review, including AI-assisted audit
            ↓
Deterministic qualification and pricing
            ↓
Quote approval and completion clearance
            ↓
Auditable payment instructions for direct funder-to-contractor payment
```

The model can help interpret submitted evidence and package a structured record. It does not set eligibility, change savings figures, alter payout limits, or authorize a disbursement. Qualification and payout caps are controlled by deterministic rules and calculations.

The public product includes a no-login designer for all 50 states and DC, with methodology offerings selected by location and available data. Milam County, Texas is the case study used to demonstrate the authenticated resident journey. Its post-acceptance milestones and settlement records remain explicitly staged.

This document intentionally omits source structure, service topology, model prompts, authentication design, storage details, and security controls.
